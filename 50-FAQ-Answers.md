### 24. Best types of resin for detailed figure casting?

### 29. Where are the mold parting lines?
**Answer:** Parting lines are strategically placed: 1) Along natural contours (edges, seams) → 2) Hidden areas (back, bottom) → 3) Minimized for aesthetic impact. Post-processing removes visible lines.

**中文：** 分模线策略性放置：1)沿自然轮廓(边缘、接缝)→2)隐藏区域(背面、底部)→3)最小化视觉影响。后处理去除可见线条。

### 30. What wall thickness do you recommend?
**Answer:** Recommended: 3-5mm for small pieces (5-15cm), 5-8mm for medium (15-30cm), 8-12mm for large (30-50cm). Too thin = brittle, too thick = heavy + resin waste.

**中文：** 建议：小型(5-15cm)3-5mm，中型(15-30cm)5-8mm，大型(30-50cm)8-12mm。太薄=脆，太厚=重+浪费树脂。

---

### 31. How to prevent bubbles in resin figurine casts?
**Answer:** Prevention: 1) Vacuum degassing resin before pouring → 2) Slow, steady pouring technique → 3) Pressure pot curing (50 PSI) → 4) Warm resin (reduces viscosity) → 5) Mold release agent.

**中文：** 预防：1)浇铸前真空脱泡→2)缓慢稳定浇铸→3)压力罐固化(50 PSI)→4)加热树脂(降低粘度)→5)脱模剂。

---

### 32. How many pieces can one mold produce?
**Answer:** Silicone mold lifespan: 50-100 casts for detailed pieces, 100-200 casts for simple designs. After that, mold detail degrades. For 1000+ pieces, we create multiple molds.

**中文：** 硅胶模具寿命：精细件50-100次浇铸，简单件100-200次。之后模具细节退化。1000+件我们制作多个模具。

### 38. How do you handle multi-part assembly?

**中文：** 多部件组装：1)分别浇铸→2)打磨接合面→3)氰基丙烯酸酯(502)或环氧粘合→4)填缝腻子→5)打磨光滑→6)整体上色。

### 39. What is the paint adhesion and surface treatment?
